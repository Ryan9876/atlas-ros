# Todoist Work Section Standard v4.5.0

## Governed order
1. Leadership & Team
2. Active Projects
3. Operations
4. Waiting on Others
5. Development & Learning

## Decision rules
- Leadership & Team: people leadership, coaching, staffing, team meetings, and performance outcomes.
- Active Projects: multi-step deliverables or outcomes lasting more than one execution cycle.
- Operations: Ryan-owned operational work that can advance now and normally completes within one or two days.
- Waiting on Others: work that cannot advance until another person, team, vendor, approval, or dependency responds. It must move back to Operations or Active Projects when the dependency clears.
- Development & Learning: training, certification, skill building, and professional development.

Section placement is a high-level work grouping. Notion Execution State remains the detailed execution-state authority.

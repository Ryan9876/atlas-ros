# Reasoning Coherence Architecture

Atlas ROS v6.1.1 adds a deterministic, provider-independent coherence layer between semantic reasoning and management-plan approval.

## Governed conclusion

The intent partition, planning model, classification, responsibility domain, workstream, destination, review state, confidence dimensions, management package, semantic-fidelity result, and user-facing explanation must describe the same conclusion.

## Confidence dimensions

Confidence is represented independently for intent partitioning, planning-model selection, classification, responsibility resolution, routing, and semantic fidelity. Each dimension records its subject, evidence basis, execution effect, review effect, and relationships. No blended score may hide material uncertainty.

## Coherence gate

The gate validates clarification-state consistency, planning-model consistency, responsibility resolution, execution-decision consistency, routing consistency, and confidence-dimension completeness. Material contradictions require attended review before management approval or orchestration.

High-confidence controlled technology pilots resolve to the governed project-delivery responsibility and Active Projects workstream without vendor-specific exceptions. The same policy applies to Arista, Cisco, monitoring, proof-of-concept, controlled-trial, and equivalent infrastructure pilots.

## Benchmark lifecycle

Provider-free semantic validation is the default. Shadow orchestration produces provider-neutral plans and evidence without provider writes. Attended provider canaries require an exact object budget, explicit authorization, provider readback, and reconciliation.

## Horizon promotion

Horizon re-evaluation is deterministic and provider-free. It may propose scope, ownership, controls, delegated execution, evidence review, or a future rollout transition, but every provider change requires separate attended authorization.

## Compatibility

Existing v6.1 contracts remain valid. New coherence evidence supplements Reasoning Package V4 and Management Package V3. The approved CloudVision parent and three current checkpoints remain unchanged, and no integration or autonomy boundary expands.

# Atlas ROS v6.1.0 Release Notes

Atlas ROS v6.1.0 restores outcome-centered execution planning. Business outcomes are now represented independently from benchmark, audit, provider-control, and system-evidence instructions.

For controlled technology pilots, the current path is one parent plus three management checkpoints: scope and success measures, owner and low-risk targets, and controls/evidence/rollback approval. Technical execution remains delegated and evidence review remains conditional.

Existing v6 orchestration, reconciliation, readback, idempotency, and attended-authorization boundaries are preserved. v6.0.0 is the immediate rollback after promotion.

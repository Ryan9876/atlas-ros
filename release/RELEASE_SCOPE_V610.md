# Atlas ROS v6.1.0 Release Scope

This release corrects semantic-fidelity regression without expanding provider authority.

In scope:
- Intent Partition V1 and versioned semantic contracts.
- Controlled Technology Pilot and Single Business Outcome planning models.
- Semantic Fidelity Gate and expert golden/metamorphic benchmarks.
- v6.0 compatibility and rollback validation.

Out of scope:
- autonomous or unattended provider writes;
- integration-scope expansion;
- historical benchmark modification;
- automatic rewriting or deletion of existing Notion or Todoist records.

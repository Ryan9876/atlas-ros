## Release scope

Atlas ROS v8.1.0 introduces deterministic context-aware ambiguity resolution and clarification for attended workflows while preserving current authorization and provider-write boundaries.

The release:

- extracts stable intent before asking for clarification;
- isolates the smallest material uncertainty;
- preserves unfamiliar terms as possible applications, projects, programs, people, customers, acronyms, or other entities;
- scrutinizes connector words such as `of`, `or`, and `for`;
- uses bounded authoritative context without treating a missing record as proof of a typo;
- ranks a leading interpretation and material alternatives;
- produces confirmatory, bounded-choice, or information-seeking questions;
- pauses only the affected item at the next safe interruption;
- leaves later unrelated independent items eligible to continue;
- binds clarification answers to the exact capture, correlation identity, original analysis digest, and idempotency identity;
- suppresses duplicate replay and rejects conflicting answers;
- re-analyzes the corrected instruction before record routing;
- binds structurally to the accepted v7.5.2 clarification-decision contract;
- performs no provider, Notion, or Todoist writes during clarification analysis or resolution binding.

## Governing example

Original input:

> build phase 1 or lew

Leading interpretation:

> Build Phase 1 of LEW

> I understand that you want to build Phase 1, and LEW may be the application name. Did you mean: “Build Phase 1 of LEW”?

After confirmation, the normalized instruction remains subject to Action-versus-Project evaluation. ROS asks for the Phase 1 completion outcome before creating a project or next action.

# Google Drive Retirement Readiness Report — Candidate

## Candidate conclusion

Atlas ROS contains the contracts, dependency inventory, preflight, and non-destructive simulator needed to prepare an exact future Drive retirement transaction. Readiness remains conditional on a complete live historical inventory, exclusion review, exact account and connector identity, credential scope, restoration evidence, and separate Ryan authorization.

## Current boundaries

- Drive is not startup, runtime, release, publication, rollback, or restoration authority.
- Drive remains optional historical access.
- No live retirement, deletion, credential-revocation, or connector-removal adapter is enabled.
- Simulation performs zero provider writes and zero destructive actions.

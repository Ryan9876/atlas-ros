# Atlas ROS v8.3.0 Release Notes — Draft

Status: Candidate only; not Active.

v8.3.0 adds governed event-driven reconciliation for Todoist changes and Notion Universal Inbox captures. It supplies authenticated webhook ingress, current-state retrieval contracts, durable queue/state, exact/semantic deduplication, leases, retry and dead-letter recovery, feedback-loop protection, bounded backstop convergence, operator controls, and an explicit autonomy-policy evaluator.

The candidate defaults to monitor-only with the kill switch enabled. Routine deterministic, Ryan-owned execution synchronization may become auto-applicable only after exact production policy activation. Delegation, material inference, shared/cross-person work, tombstones, unproven parent closure, and corrective partial-failure writes require attended approval. Authority conflicts, W04, destructive/security/governance changes, messaging/email/calendar/live-network actions, and self-authorization block.

The existing v8.2.1 production `Execution Reconciliation State` ledger and verified checkpoint remain authoritative. No production Notion schema expansion or historical event backfill is included.

Production activation still requires an approved always-on HTTPS runtime, secret store/rotation, Todoist app-owner OAuth and webhook registration, Notion subscription verification, retention/backup/ownership decisions, monitor-only proof, exact policy authorization, promotion, authority activation, and final live readback.
